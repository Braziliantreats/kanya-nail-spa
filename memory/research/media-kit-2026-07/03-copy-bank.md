## Fit-line formula (forced input at onboarding)
`"[%] [demographic] in [location] who [behavior/interest]"`
Weak: "Helping you work smarter and live better." Strong: "Strength training for busy moms over 35 — 78% women 25–34, US, above-average household income."

## Headline variants (buyer language, cited)
1. *"Don't make it a guessing game."* Rates scoped. Rights priced. Results attached. (2017-source phrase [C087/C093] — fine as copy, NEVER cite as research.)
2. *"Hard to justify the cost"* is why deals die — this kit is the justification. Proof first, stats verified today, one screenshot from approved. [C047]
3. The laborious part of creator marketing is vetting and paperwork [C044] — this page does both: fit, proof, scope, terms, one scroll.

## Verified quotes — usable in marketing (all named, dated, real publications)
- "Follower count survives because it's easy to put in a spreadsheet" (paraphrase of Later's GM of Creator, Apr 2026 [C107]) — perfect for brand-side landing copy.
- Rights-scope-defined-upfront improves deal conversion — TrueRights CEO Woollams [C106] — the rights-booster's marketing spine.
- Comment quality predicts campaign performance better than "anything on a media kit" — talent manager, NetInfluencer roundup [C108].
- Tinuiti EVP on needing to validate value to justify cost [C047]; Ruggable CMO on the "laborious/administrative" burden [C044]; Duolingo CMO on reach-strategy dying [C046]; TheRealReal on audience relevance [C040].
- Verify each verbatim at source before it ships in copy — the Digiday/Modern Retail set was confirmed real but not all were re-fetched in the final session.

## BANNED — never use, anywhere, ever
- **"78% (or 72/76/87%) of brands request a media kit"** — fabricated content-farm output with rotating numbers and rotating attributions; the actual IMH 2026 report contains no media-kit question at all [C102][C103].
- **"Brands spend 12 minutes / 10–15 minutes reading kits"** and any specific read-time stat [C097]. Planning assumption stays the two-stage scan (5–10s pass/fail → minutes if passed), cited as a model, never as a number.
- **"78% of kits are interactive," "89%/64%" adoption stats** [C089][C090] — same content farm.
- **"1–2 of my last 50 deals asked for a kit"** as a marketing stat — real quote but single-voice, Dec 2022 [C094]; use only as an internal directional signal.
- Any InfluenceFlow-sourced number [C097]. Any "adversarial vote" tag from the old study.
- Vendor speed claims ("4x more responses," "10x faster") [C074][C079] — quarantined.

## Tone rules for kit copy
Specific beats generic everywhere. No "I love working with brands." No "authentic/passionate" filler. Every claim dated. The kit speaks the buyer's language (CPM, ER vs. range, usage windows) — not creator-community language.
