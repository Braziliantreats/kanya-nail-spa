# 01 — How the Market Actually Works

## The two markets [SOLID]
| | INFLUENCER deal | UGC deal |
|---|---|---|
| Sells | Audience access / reach | Content for the brand's own channels & ads |
| Priced on | Fit × engagement × niche × deliverable | Per deliverable + usage rights; followers mostly irrelevant |
| Brand-side buyer | Brand / social / comms teams | Paid-media / performance teams (often a different budget) [C007] |
| Proof that closes | Audience fit + engagement quality + past campaign results | Work samples with ad metrics (CTR, hook retention, ROAS) |
| The artifact | Media kit | Portfolio (work-sample library) |
| Typical money | Micro $200–2K/post; tier medians $500 → $16.8K [C065][C066] | $150–500/video typical; asking $198 vs. PAID $178 [C061][C100] |

Budget ownership inside brands is actively contested (comms vs. social vs. paid vs. commerce) [C001]. Spend is real and growing (Unilever: social to 50% of ad budget, ~300K creators [C002]; influencer share of Cyber Week orders nearly doubled YoY [C031]).

## The buyer journey [SOLID]
discovery → shortlist (10–15 creators) → vetting → **internal approval** → negotiation → brief/contract → delivery → measurement → renew/drop

**Vetting criteria, ranked (2024–26 practitioner consensus):**
1. Audience fit vs. the brand's customer [C040] — the first check, and the fastest disqualifier
2. Engagement QUALITY — comment quality, saves/shares, consistency of recent views (median of last 10–20 posts is the buyers' most-cited leading indicator [C105])
3. Content/brand-safety adjacency (scan last 10 posts)
4. Past results — including **branded-vs-organic performance dropoff**, now checked before pricing [C105]
5. Authenticity (bot checks; ~1 in 4 creators fails professional vetting [C013])
6. Follower count — a spreadsheet-convenience filter only. Best-corroborated finding in the project (50 named professionals [C105]; real IMH survey structure [C102]; brand CMO quotes [C040][C046]).

**The real reading environment:** kits are opened mostly on mobile [C011], skimmed not read (two-stage model: 5–10s pass/fail, then minutes IF passed — no credible hard number exists, treat all read-time stats as lore), and the numbers frequently get re-typed into an internal **spreadsheet/scorecard** for comparison [C105][C107]. Machine-copyable numbers matter as much as pretty layout.

**Contracts & money friction [SOLID]:** usage rights priced separately (+30–50% for 6-mo, +100–150% perpetual [C007][C014]; measured average +39.9% [C100]); whitelisting/Spark +20–50% [C037]; net-60/90 payment terms endemic and the top relationship-killer (56% of creators have been paid late [C017]); revision-limit fights (2 rounds is the standard fix); FTC penalties up to $53,088/violation [C009]. Rights-scope defined UPFRONT is a named predictor of deal conversion [C106].

**Re-booking:** repeat relationships are where money consolidates (41.5% marketplace repeat benchmark [C041]; ambassador re-sign 60–80% [C042]). The artifact that wins the NEXT deal is the **post-campaign report** [C094][C105].

## The seller funnel [SOLID]
New sellers live on outbound; inbound grows with reputation. What works: short, brand-first, personalized pitches to the actual decision-maker, proof line + portfolio link in the body, follow-up at 5–7 days [C062][C063]. ~75% of pitch emails are never opened [C064] — the kit converts interest; it does not create it.

## Where the kit matters — and doesn't [SOLID]
- **Kit matters:** cold outbound; mid-market influencer vetting; any deal where a marketer must forward proof upward to unlock budget.
- **Kit is skipped:** warm rebooks (post-campaign report is the artifact — Moore: ~1–2 kit requests in his last ~50 deals [C094 — single voice, Dec 2022, directional only]); TikTok Shop/affiliate (Shop profile is the artifact; standalone Creator Marketplace being deprecated for US Shop [C033]); high-volume marketplace UGC (profile + ratings).
- **No credible number exists for "% of brands that request a kit."** The circulating 78% figure is fabricated [BANNED — see 03-copy-bank.md]. Product implication: route users honestly by tier (see 06-positioning.md).
