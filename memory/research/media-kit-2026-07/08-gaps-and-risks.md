# 08 — Gaps & Risks (read before building)

## A. The two load-bearing bets that are NOT proven
These are the spec's flashiest ideas and its two least-evidenced ones. Ship both instrumented; neither is settled.

1. **Section ordering (fit-first vs. proof-first).** No one has data — not us, not competitors, not the experts. The entire market's revealed preference is stats-first; the expert advice chain for proof-first traces heavily to ONE voice (Justin Moore) amplified across outlets. Resolution: the month-one A/B. Severity if wrong: moderate (content survives; order is tunable).
2. **The Snapshot / internal-forward card.** Nobody in three research tracks has ever seen a real internal approval one-pager — the design is a reconstruction from descriptions. Marketers may copy-paste into their own template or spreadsheet instead of forwarding a card. Resolution: DM question #1 (get 5 redacted real ones) + copy-button instrumentation. Severity if wrong: the feature pivots to "copyable text summary," cheap.

## B. Questions the research could not answer (no credible data exists)
- % of brands that request a kit at all (the only circulating number was fabricated).
- Actual kit read-time, read-order, and device split (all circulating figures are vendor lore).
- Whether visible pricing helps or hurts INFLUENCER-side response rates (genuinely contested; UGC side is settled).
- DM vs. email response rates for cold pitches.
- Deal-source split (inbound/outbound/marketplace %) for consistently-booked creators.
- True earnings distribution for creators (job-aggregator figures are junk; practitioner ranges are estimates).
- Exact per-tier brand budget sizes and cycle timing.
→ All routed to `07-research-plan.md`. Do not let sample-kit copy or marketing imply answers to these.

## C. Business-model gaps (research covered the product, not the business)
- **Willingness-to-pay is unproven for every booster.** Anchors exist (Beacons $30 tier) but no direct purchase evidence for a case-study builder as a paid feature. Flagship booster has an explicit fallback (move to free) — decide the trigger metric in advance.
- **Booster pricing levels were never tested** — the research ranks value, not price points.
- **The manager/agency segment is unaddressed:** managers run 10–40 creators and buy per-roster (Pillar has a tier for a reason). A per-creator free product may be leaving the most monetizable segment on the table. Deliberate decision needed: serve or skip.
- **Competitive response is unplanned:** the moat is done-for-you defaults, but Beacons could ship opinionated proof-first defaults in a quarter. What's the answer if they do? (Likely: the service layer + honesty positioning — but nobody has written this down.)
- **Unit economics of "done-for-you" are unexamined:** who does the homework per creator, at what cost, at what volume does it break?

## D. Scope gaps (out of research scope, still real)
- **Geography:** everything is US-DTC. UK/EU (ASA/CMA disclosure), non-DTC verticals, B2B — unknown.
- **Legal:** contract/rights templates (booster #2) need a real lawyer before shipping; FTC figure needs verification at ftc.gov.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 budget flush / Q1 drought will distort any booking-rate metric; no baseline data collected.
- **Gifting→paid ladder:** how nano creators actually enter the market — unmapped; may matter for onboarding copy.
- **Platform risk:** TikTok marketplace mechanics changed mid-project [C033]; the no-kit-tier routing copy depends on it — quarterly re-check.

## F. The pre-mortem (if booking rates don't improve, in order of likelihood)
1. **Distribution, not artifact.** The kit converts interest; most creators have no qualified viewers. Mitigation: onboarding teaches pitching; analytics-driven follow-up nudges; honest expectations copy.
2. **Cold-start proof gap.** Newest creators (our likeliest adopters) have zero case studies; an empty proof-first kit looks worse than a stats page. Mitigation: zero-deal wizard and UGC preset at LAUNCH.
3. **The ordering bet is wrong or unmeasured.** Mitigation: instruments + A/B from day one — never ship blind.
