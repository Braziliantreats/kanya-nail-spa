Ranking principle: boosters that address a documented deal-death point AND that the *buyer* feels outrank boosters that flatter the creator.

## Free tier (table stakes + acquisition)
- Daily-fresh verified stats (Beacons gives this free [C095] — matching is mandatory, it is NOT a differentiator).
- Smart links; lightweight invoice + terms strip (addresses payment-friction deaths #4/#6 without building a payments platform).
- Profile-hygiene checklist + fit-line wizard at onboarding.

## Kill list (survived the stress test — stay dead)
- Press-kit PDF as a product (kit-export button allowed).
- Standalone testimonials section (testimonials live inside case cards only).
- Paywalled stat-freshness (Pillar's model [C098]; Beacons already broke it [C095]).
- Email-gating the kit.
- AI send-volume outreach tools (volume pitching is the documented failure mode [C062][C064]).
- Full CRM/pipeline tooling; payments/escrow rail (Passionfroot's capital-intensive moat [C082]); e-signature platform (commodity).

## Unvetted product ideas from the old study (NOT in lineup — evaluate separately)
- **"Verified" audience badge** via HypeAuditor/Modash API partnership — addresses the 56.5% fraud concern [C102] but requires partnership cost/feasibility check [GAP].
- **"Kit Score"** completeness gamification — plausible mitigation for "creators don't fill fields," zero evidence either way [GAP].
