# 06 — Positioning: The Two-Sided Edge

## The gap (verified, F8)
Every competitor's *marketing* says the buyer matters; every *rendered kit* in the wild still opens with follower counts and unlabeled logo walls — even where the proof feature technically exists in the product [C071][C075][C076][C077][C096]. The gap is a **defaults-and-behavior gap**: DIY tools ship the field; creators don't do the homework. A done-for-you service closes it structurally — we do the homework. A SaaS builder can't copy that without becoming a service.

Supporting signal: the category's moats are shallower than they look — one competitor claims 13,580 creators but shows 36 live kits [C096]. Published-kit rates are tiny across the category.

## The one-liner
**"The free, done-for-you media kit that renders as the buyer's approval packet — and tells you when you don't need one."**

## The 60-second pitch (post-stress-test version)
> MayhemTBD builds you the approval packet, not another stats page. Free, done-for-you: we take your accounts and your past collabs and hand back a live kit that opens with structured results a marketer can defend to their boss — deliverable, reach, ER vs. your tier's range, conversions — stats verified and dated today, a scoped offer menu with usage and whitelisting priced in, and a one-tap summary they can drop straight into the email that gets you approved. Every other tool gives you fields and hopes you fill them; the kits actually live on those tools open with follower counts and dead logo walls — we checked. And if you're at the tier where kits don't matter — warm rebooks, TikTok Shop, marketplace UGC — we'll tell you that too, and give you the post-campaign report that wins the *next* deal instead. Built by a conformance-review shop: your kit passes the checks brands actually run — loads in link previews, reads on a phone in one scroll, every claim dated and sourced.

## What no competitor our size is doing (build/say list)
1. Structured case cards with live tier-benchmark context — nobody renders them.
2. Branded-vs-organic performance line — buyers now check the #ad dropoff before pricing [C105]; nobody surfaces it; trivial to compute from synced data.
3. Rights/whitelisting menu inside the kit — universal absence.
4. The copyable Snapshot summary — CreatorsJet sells the verb ("forward and approve"), nobody ships the noun [C075] [PROVISIONAL artifact — see gaps].
5. Server-rendered kits as a stated reliability feature — Pillar's JS-only failure is the proof point [C081].
6. Honest tier-routing ("you may need receipts, not a kit") — converts the kits-are-dead threat into credibility.
7. Spreadsheet-friendly copyable numbers — the actual reading environment [C105][C107].

- Steelman "creators buy vanity, not buyer-value": the done-for-you mechanic means the creator gets the buyer-optimized kit *without doing the work* — adoption rides on effort saved, not on caring about buyers.
- Steelman "new creators have no proof": the zero-deal case-study wizard and UGC sample-with-metrics preset are LAUNCH features, not fast-follows (pre-mortem cause #2).
