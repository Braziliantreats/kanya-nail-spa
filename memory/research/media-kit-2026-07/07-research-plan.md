## The 6 DM questions for brand-side marketers
1. Last creator you booked cold — what did you actually forward internally to get sign-off? Can you share it redacted?
2. When a kit shows rate ranges vs. "rates on request," which do you prefer for a first look — and has visible pricing ever stopped you from reaching out?
3. What's the first thing you check that disqualifies a creator in under 10 seconds?
4. Have usage rights or whitelisting ever stalled a deal you wanted to do — at what step?
5. Roughly how long did you spend on the last media kit you opened, and on what device?
6. Do you ever request a media kit before first contact — and when was the last time you did? (Settles the question the fabricated 78% stat pretended to answer.)

## The booking-rate A/B (month one, with our own creators)
- **Variant A:** fit-first + proof-teaser default order. **Variant B:** proof-literally-first. Identical content.
- **Metric:** kit-view → qualified inquiry (form submit or reply-tracked email), 60 days.
- **Sample logic:** at ~5% baseline inquiry rate, detecting a jump to ~10% needs roughly 400–450 views per arm → ~900 tracked views total (e.g., 30 creators averaging 30 views), or run longer. Smaller samples only detect huge effects — report as directional.
- Secondary instrumentation regardless of arm: scroll depth, section clicks, Snapshot copy-button usage, time to first inquiry.
- Beware seasonality when reading results (Q4 flush / Q1 drought) — compare arms concurrently, never across periods.

## Landing-page test
A/B the positioning with creators: "built for the brand reader / approval packet" framing vs. a conventional "beautiful media kit in minutes" framing. Metric: signup rate. This tests the adoption steelman directly.
